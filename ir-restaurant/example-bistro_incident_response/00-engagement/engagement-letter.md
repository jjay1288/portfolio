## 1. Purpose of Engagement

The Client has requested that the Consultant provide security incident response services in connection with a confirmed compromise of the Client's website, example-bistro.invalid, hosted on SharedHost Provider shared hosting. Work commenced March 10, 2026 following verbal authorization from the Client. This letter formalizes the terms of that engagement retroactively from the effective date above.

---

## 2. Authorization

The Client hereby authorizes the Consultant to:

- Access, examine, and copy files and data associated with the example-bistro.invalid website and its hosting environment (SharedHost Provider account under the domain example-bistro.invalid)
- Access the SharedHost Provider hosting panel using credentials provided by or on behalf of the Client
- Access the WordPress administration panel at example-bistro.invalid using credentials provided by or on behalf of the Client
- Access the site's database using credentials provided by or on behalf of the Client
- Retain forensic copies of malicious files and evidence artifacts for the duration of this engagement and for a period of one (1) year following case closure, after which they will be securely deleted unless the Client requests earlier destruction
- Perform remediation actions on the site as described in the Statement of Work and pre-eradication checklist, including but not limited to: removing malicious files, replacing WordPress core files, deleting malicious database records, rotating credentials, and modifying access configurations

**The Consultant is NOT authorized to:**

- Access any systems, accounts, or data outside the scope defined above without explicit additional written authorization from the Client
- Share case materials, findings, or client information with any third party except as required by law or as necessary to engage hosting provider support on the Client's behalf
- Take any action on live production systems that has not been pre-approved through the eradication checklist process or explicit Client instruction

## 3. Scope of Work

The full scope of services is defined in the accompanying Statement of Work (`00-engagement/statement-of-work.md`). In summary, services include:

- Forensic investigation of the website compromise
- Malware identification, collection, and analysis
- Attack chain reconstruction
- Evidence preservation
- Eradication of all identified malicious artifacts
- Post-eradication verification
- Technical and client-facing reporting
- Breach notification assessment and drafting

## 6. Confidentiality

The Consultant agrees to treat all information obtained during this engagement as confidential, including but not limited to: site credentials, user information, business operations data, customer data, and findings of the investigation. The Consultant will not disclose this information to any third party except:

- As required by applicable law or a valid court order
- To the hosting provider (SharedHost Provider) solely as necessary to facilitate remediation, and only with information minimally necessary for that purpose
- With the express written consent of the Client

The Client acknowledges that the Consultant may reference this engagement in general terms (e.g., "incident response for a Washington DC restaurant") for professional portfolio purposes, without identifying the Client by name or disclosing any specific findings.

---

## 7. Data Handling and Evidence Retention

Forensic evidence collected during this engagement (malware samples, credential dumps, log extracts) will be:

- Stored in the password-protected case file repository for the duration of the engagement
- Retained for one (1) year following case closure for potential legal or insurance purposes
- Securely deleted after the retention period unless the Client requests an extension in writing

The Consultant will not use forensic evidence for any purpose other than this engagement. Credential data recovered during forensic analysis (e.g., captured WordPress passwords) will not be stored in plaintext beyond what is necessary for the investigation record, and will be redacted in any externally shared documents.
